Frequently asked questions

What is CruxCut?

CruxCut is an AI video editor for iPhone that turns raw climbing footage into a follow-cam edit, Crux Highlights, and a shareable Route Sticker.

Can I use CruxCut for both lead climbing and bouldering?

Yes. CruxCut works with both lead climbing and bouldering footage, indoors or outdoors.

What if the AI loses track of the climber?

Draw the climbing route and CruxCut will identify the climber again and refine the tracking path.

Are my videos uploaded to a server?

No. Analysis and editing happen directly on your iPhone.
