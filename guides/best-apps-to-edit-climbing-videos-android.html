Best Apps to Edit Climbing Videos on Android

Looking for an app to edit climbing videos on your Android phone? Compare tracking follow-cams and general editors by the job you need done, based on their Google Play listings.

Published November 21, 2026 · By the CruxCut team

Disclosure: CruxCut publishes this page. We describe each app only as far as its official Google Play listing does, say what that listing does not mention, and point to a general editor when it may fit better.

Start with the problem you want the app to solve

Most climbing clips need one of three things: a small climber turned into a clear vertical clip, the useful moves found inside a long recording, or a finished edit with trimming, speed changes, and captions. Android has apps for each of these jobs, and they are not interchangeable. Pick the app by the job that costs you the most time, then test it on one real attempt.

Choose by the job you need done

The climber looks tiny in a wide or tripod shot

Start with an app that describes subject tracking, such as CruxCut or BoulderCam. Both describe automatically moving the crop to follow the subject. A general editor can zoom in, but you would need to place the crop yourself and keep it moving with keyframes. If the crop loses the climber, see why auto-tracking loses the climber.

You need to find the moves worth sharing

Of the five listings, only CruxCut describes climbing highlights built around the crux. If that is your bottleneck, read how automatic climbing highlights work and what they cannot decide for you.

You already picked the clips and want to polish them

CapCut, InShot, and VN describe broader manual tools: speed changes, keyframes, captions, and multiple layers. They fit best after you have chosen and framed the moments. You can use a tracking app first and a general editor second, but check each app's export before layering another edit on top of it.

Test any app with one real attempt

  • Use a clip where the climber is small, then watch the whole preview for moments where hands or feet leave the frame.
  • Check that the exported file keeps the aspect ratio your platform expects. A tall crop can cut off feet, as explained in cropping vertically without cutting off hands or feet.
  • Keep the setup and the finish in the trim, using these trim checkpoints.
  • Look at the in-app purchase and ads disclosures on the listing, and see what the free version exports before you build a workflow around it.
  • Keep the original recording until you have watched the exported copy from start to finish.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best app to edit climbing videos on Android?

It depends on the job. CruxCut and BoulderCam describe automatic climber or subject tracking, CruxCut also describes climbing highlights, and CapCut, InShot, and VN are general editors with broader manual tools. Try one real clip before choosing.

Can an Android app keep the climber centered automatically?

CruxCut and BoulderCam both describe automatically tracking the subject and keeping it centered in the crop on their Google Play listings. The listings do not promise the result for every wall, camera angle, or crowded gym, so check the preview before exporting.

Do I need a climbing-specific editor, or is a general editor enough?

A general editor is enough when you already chose the clips and mainly need trimming, speed changes, or captions. A climbing-specific tool helps most when the climber is small in a wide or tripod shot and you want the frame to follow them.

Is CruxCut available on Android?

Yes. CruxCut has a Google Play listing for Android phones. Availability can depend on your device and country, so check the listing on your phone before planning a workflow around it.
