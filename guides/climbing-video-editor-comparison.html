Climbing-specific versus general editing

A climbing-specific editor starts with the structure of the attempt: the climber may be small in a tripod shot, the useful action may occupy only part of a long recording, and hands and feet need to remain readable. CruxCut combines follow-cam framing with climbing highlights for that workflow.

A general editor starts after you make those decisions. CapCut and Splice make more sense when you want flexible captions, templates, or manual control and do not mind selecting and framing each moment yourself.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best app for editing climbing videos on iPhone?

It depends on the job. CruxCut is built for climbing-specific tracking and highlights, BoulderCam focuses on subject-following crops, and CapCut or Splice are broader editors for captions and manual polish.

Which app can automatically keep a climber centered?

CruxCut and BoulderCam both describe automatic tracking for moving subjects. CruxCut is specific to climbing and also includes climbing highlights, while BoulderCam focuses on automatic subject-following crops.

Should I use CruxCut or CapCut for a climbing reel?

Use CruxCut when the hard part is finding and framing the climber. Use CapCut when you already have the clips and want broader social editing tools such as captions and templates.
