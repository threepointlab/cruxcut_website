How to Automatically Create Climbing Highlights on iPhone

Use AI to find the difficult and dynamic parts of your attempts, then choose the moments you want in one share-ready climbing reel.

Published August 28, 2026 · By the cruxcut team

The short answer: record the whole climb, let a climbing-specific editor find movement-based sections, then keep the crux, send, or fall that tells the story.

What “automatic highlights” means

Automatic highlight tools reduce the time spent scrubbing long attempts. CruxCut analyzes video on your iPhone to surface difficult and dynamic sections of the climb. You choose the moments to keep, so the final reel still reflects what mattered in your session.

This is different from blindly shortening a video. A useful climbing highlight keeps enough setup to understand the move, the movement itself, and—when it matters—the finish or reaction.

A four-step iPhone workflow

  1. Record the full attempt. Use a stable wide shot that includes the start, feet, crux, and finish.
  2. Analyze the clip. CruxCut looks at the climb's movement and organizes crux-worthy sections for review.
  3. Pick the story. Keep one hard move, a clean send, a fall-and-send sequence, or highlights across several climbs.
  4. Export for the destination. Use a vertical result for Reels or Shorts, and check that captions do not cover hands or feet.

Before you export

  • The highlight begins close to the action but still has context.
  • The climber remains readable on a phone screen.
  • The crux is not hidden by captions or a tight crop.
  • The ending makes sense: control, finish, fall, or reaction.
  • The aspect ratio matches where you plan to share it.

Frequently asked questions

Is there an app that finds highlights in climbing videos?

Yes. CruxCut analyzes climbing movement on iPhone to surface difficult and dynamic sections, then you choose the moments to keep as short clips or a multi-climb highlight reel.

How do I trim a climbing video to just the crux?

Start with the full attempt, review the movement-based sections, and keep the difficult or dynamic sequence that tells the story. Leave a little context before and after the crux so the move is easy to understand.

Can tripod footage become an Instagram climbing reel?

Yes. A stable wide shot gives the editor the full climb. CruxCut can follow the climber, help surface highlight moments, and produce a vertical result suited to Reels or Shorts.

Does CruxCut process climbing videos on a server?

No processing-server upload is required. Video analysis and editing happen on your iPhone.
